## About

I am a Civil Engineer working at the intersection of **climate-resilient water infrastructure**, **hydrological machine learning**, and **satellite-based environmental monitoring** — with particular focus on the Bay of Bengal delta. My current role at **Tetra Private Limited** leads multi-partner coastal water and WASH initiatives across six climate-vulnerable districts of southern Bangladesh, in collaboration with BRAC, Welthungerhilfe, ANANDO, and Innovations Against Poverty.

Alongside this applied work, I maintain an active research portfolio spanning **spatiotemporal salinity dynamics**, **compound flood risk under future climate scenarios**, **physics-constrained deep learning for flood prediction**, and **community-based anticipatory action**. I am especially interested in graduate research positions where these threads can be drawn together — translating climate-model uncertainty into actionable infrastructure decisions for utility operators and resilience planners.

## Research Interests

- Climate-resilient water infrastructure in deltaic and coastal-vulnerable contexts
- Hydrological machine learning — transformer architectures, physics-informed neural networks, extreme-event prediction
- Compound flood risk and multi-driver hazard analysis under CMIP6 SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5 scenarios
- Satellite remote sensing — Landsat, Sentinel SAR, NDSI / LULC change detection
- Community-based anticipatory action and last-mile early-warning for humanitarian WASH
- Geospatial multi-criteria analysis for renewable energy siting and adaptation planning
